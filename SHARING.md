# Share Color Study

Color Study is a free browser lab and open collection for exploring surviving color in painted surfaces and textiles. Compare a real photograph with a labeled mathematical enhancement, inspect native-resolution details, and keep source credits and reproducible settings. False color does not reconstruct the original palette.

[Open Color Study](https://color-study-painted-surfaces.kristen368163.chatgpt.site/) · [Code and image files](https://github.com/kristencline-arch/color-study) · [NASA Spinoff inspiration](https://spinoff.nasa.gov/Manipulating_Satellite_Photos_Now_Reveals_Ancient_Images)

These links have photographic social previews. Keep the original and enhanced labels, credit and source license when reposting an image. Each record includes its own terms; the code’s MIT license does not replace image licenses. The software is independent of NASA, DStretch, museums and the photographers.

Have a photograph of painted plaster, dyed cloth, a manuscript or surviving polychromy? The contribution form publishes photographs immediately under CC BY 4.0 with an explicit consent step and a report link. Opening a personal photograph in the lab keeps it in the browser until the visitor deliberately uses the separate publishing form.
